<product_requirements>
The Centro de Monitoreo Inteligente DAMI (originally DAMI Political Intelligence Center) is a full-stack application designed for real-time political intelligence. Initial requirements included a responsive dashboard with dynamic AI recommendations (Radar, Heat Map, Sr. X Feed, IA Táctica), JWT-based multi-role authentication (Admin, Analyst, Operator), data monitoring (political actors, territorial heat maps, social media feeds, alerts), and AI/automation (DAMI Bot, advanced AI like deepfake, autonomous agents, predictive, emotional AI).

User feedback led to critical expansions: the Centro de Comando Real provides highly specific, real-time, actionable insights for Frente Renovador de la Concordia Social, including severity and recommended steps. A real-time territorial map of Misiones (78 municipalities) with traffic light activity indicators for social media/media influence on Frente Renovador was also requested. Recent additions include a Centro Estadístico for positive/negative social activity sentiment and an Informe Diario for summaries and suggestions, requiring real-time social media API integrations (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube), automatic alerts, and competitive analysis.
</product_requirements>
