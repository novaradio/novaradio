<product_requirements>
The Centro de Monitoreo Inteligente DAMI is a political intelligence dashboard for Frente Renovador in Misiones, initially an MVP with responsive UI, AI recommendations, JWT auth, and general data monitoring. It evolved into a Centro de Comando Real with severity indicators, a Misiones territorial map, Centro Estadístico, Informe Diario, and real-time social media integrations (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube). A Módulo de Encuestas Sociales Predictivas was added. The core goal is a unified, predictive, 100% analytical system, deeply integrating AI for Misiones' political data. Recent work consolidated modules into a Centro de Inteligencia Predictiva that is visually simple yet analytically advanced, offering explanatory, synthetic, statistical, and scalable insights without technical jargon, tailored for the Misiones 2025 elections with a focus on Oscar Herrera Ahuad and the Ley de Lemas.
</product_requirements>
